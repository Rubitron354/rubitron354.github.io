Privacy Policy for Ultron

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Ultron is a personal automation application created by Adam McMillen and used only by him, on his own computer and with his own Google account. There are no other users.

Information the application accesses

With the owner's consent through Google OAuth, Ultron accesses the owner's own Google Drive so that his personal software agents can read, create, and organize his own documents as part of his private projects.

How information is used

Information accessed through Google APIs is used only to carry out the owner's own tasks on his own systems. It is not sold, rented, shared with, or disclosed to any third party, and it is not used for advertising.

Data storage

Ultron stores an OAuth access token on the owner's own computer so the application can act on his behalf between runs. Documents remain in the owner's own Google Drive and on his own computer.

Your choices

The owner can revoke Ultron's access at any time from his Google account's security settings, which immediately stops the application's access.
