Ultron

Ultron is a personal automation tool built and operated by Adam McMillen for his own private use. It runs on his own computer and connects to his own Google account, with his consent, so his personal software agents can read and file documents in his own Google Drive while they help him with his projects.

Ultron is not a public service. It has no other users, is not offered or sold to anyone, and cannot be signed up for.

How Ultron handles information is described in its privacy policy.
